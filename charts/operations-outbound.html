FLOW OF COMMUNICATION
1
Team Members / Executives
Execute tasks and raise requests or issues to their Team Lead.
2
Process Coordinator (PC)
PC reviews, verifies, prioritizes and escalates if required.
3
Manager / TL Layer
Managers / TLs work on only important things. Managers / TLs review & take necessary action.
4
Executive Assistants (EA)
They are the voice of the Directors. EAs consolidate and compile the information and present it to Directors.
NOTES &
GUIDELINES
A
All communication and escalation must follow the defined flow. Direct communication with Directors or Managers is not allowed.
B
PC (Process Coordinator) is the primary control and escalation authority.
C
Verbal / WhatsApp escalations are not allowed and may lead to negative impact on performance.
D
PC layer ensures quality control, paper workflow, and prevents unnecessary escalations.
E
Use the central system (FMS / Dashboard) to raise requests, track status, and share updates.
F
All tasks and projects will be assigned only through the Executive Assistant or the respective Department Team Lead. In departments where no Team Lead is assigned, task delegation will be managed solely by the EA. Executive and Senior Executive level employees are not authorized to delegate tasks within their departments.
